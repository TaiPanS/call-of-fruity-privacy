CALL OF FRUITE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

The Sixth Hammer OOD, a company established in Bulgaria, is the publisher and data controller for Call Of Fruity (Android package com.call.fruity). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game, contact us, or visit this website.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, unlocked fruits and upgrades, in-game currency, preferences, selected arena, player display name, and simulated leaderboard information locally on your device. This lets the game remember your progress and settings. The full local save is not uploaded to our servers, and no account or sign-in is required to play. You can remove local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game, subject to your device backup settings.

Statistics and analytics

We use Voodoo's TinySauce integration to understand how the game is used and performs. TinySauce may send the following information from the game to Voodoo and make statistics available to us:

InformationExamples
IdentifiersApp-instance or installation identifiers, user/device identifiers generated by the SDK, and—depending on the device, permissions, SDK configuration, and your choices—the Android advertising identifier.
Gameplay and engagementSessions, gameplay events, progression events, feature use, level or match outcomes, and other gameplay metrics. These are selected analytics events, not your full local save.
Device and technical dataDevice model, operating-system and app version, language, performance information, diagnostic logs, crash details, memory state, and stack traces.
Approximate locationCountry-level location inferred from technical information such as the IP address. We do not request precise GPS location for analytics.

We use this information to measure player activity, understand feature use, improve and balance the game, run product experiments or A/B tests, forecast product metrics, and detect, diagnose, and fix crashes or performance problems. We do not use TinySauce statistics to show personalized advertising in Call Of Fruity or to sell your personal information for money.

Who receives analytics data

Voodoo processes the analytics information through its systems and service providers and may share relevant gameplay information, technical logs, and analytics with us as its partner studio. Voodoo describes its processing, recipients, and privacy choices in the Voodoo Privacy Policy. We may also disclose information where required by law or when necessary to protect users, the game, or our legal rights.

Legal bases and your choices

Where applicable law requires consent for optional analytics or access to an identifier, we rely on your consent. In other circumstances, we may rely on our legitimate interests in understanding use of the game, improving it, and keeping it stable and secure, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights. You may refuse or withdraw consent through any privacy choice shown in the game. You can also reset or restrict the Android advertising identifier in your device settings. Refusing optional analytics does not prevent use of the game's core offline features.

Retention

Voodoo states that analytics data may be retained for up to 25 months after your last activity and is then deleted or anonymized. Crash and other technical data is kept only as long as necessary for its purpose or to meet legal obligations. We keep analytics reports available to us only as long as reasonably necessary for product improvement, security, and legal compliance. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to respond and meet applicable legal requirements.

International transfers and security

Voodoo, Google, GitHub, and our service providers may process information in countries outside your own. Where required, the relevant provider uses recognized safeguards for international transfers. We and our providers use reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; and complain to a data protection authority. To exercise a right concerning information controlled by The Sixth Hammer OOD, email contact@thesixthhammer.com. For information controlled by Voodoo, contact dpo@voodoo.io or use the options described in Voodoo's policy. We may need information sufficient to verify and locate the relevant record.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly ask children to provide their name, email address, or other directly identifying information inside the game. If local law requires parental consent for analytics, a child must not enable analytics without that consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information without the required permission, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may independently process installation, security, diagnostic, device, and backup information under their own settings and policies. Google Play may provide us with app performance and crash reports. See Google's Privacy Policy.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, resolve issues, protect the game, and maintain necessary support records. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store correspondence. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visit information, including your IP address, to operate and secure its hosting service. We have not added advertising, tracking scripts, or analytics to this site. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game, its integrations, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, contact The Sixth Hammer OOD, Bulgaria, at contact@thesixthhammer.com.
